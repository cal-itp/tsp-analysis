# Definitions

## Near-side stop
A stop that is sufficiently close to a signal that it complicates when a TSP request should be made. If a bus lane or queue jump is present, we define a near-side stop to be a stop that is further than 20m before a stop line at a signalized intersection. If a bus lane is not present, we define a near-side stop to be a stop that is less than 100m before a stop line at a signalized intersection

## TSP area
The City of Culver City

## Pedestrian Signal
A signal that exclusively facilitates pedestrian crossing, as determined through analyst judgement using Google Maps Streetview
